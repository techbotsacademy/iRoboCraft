Create an Arduino program to control a 4-joint robotic arm using four servo motors.

- Base (Pin 3): Turn left and right.
- Shoulder (Pin 5): Move up and down.
- Elbow (Pin 7): Bend forward and backward.
- Claw (Pin 9): Open and close.

First, move all servos to 90 degrees. Then move the base, shoulder and elbow one by one between 60° and 120°, returning to their starting positions. Finally, open the claw to 40° and close it to 90°.

Use the Servo library, for loops and small delays for smooth movement. Repeat the movements continuously. Add simple comments explaining the program.
